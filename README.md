# Rankup

When you learn a higher rank of a spell, Rankup puts it on your action bars where the old rank was.

- Automatic: runs a moment after you learn a spell, level up or log in. It waits until combat ends.
- Window: every spell on your bars, which ones can rank up, and a history of what was swapped.
- Keep a rank: click a spell in the window to keep it at the rank you put on the bar.
- Toast, chat line and sound when ranks change (each can be turned off).
- Only spells are touched. Macros, items and anything else on your bars are left alone.

Open the window from the minimap button. Right-click the button (or type `/rankup`) to rank up right now. Shift-click toggles automatic mode. Ctrl-click opens the issue log.

If the game refuses a swap, Rankup says so once and does not retry in a loop.
